Tracking sensitive information through the system

⚠️ Critical Security Boundary
Once PII enters the Encryption Layer, it is transformed into encrypted shares. From this point forward, no single entity—not the backend, not individual MPC nodes, not even the Solana blockchain—ever sees the original plaintext "Albert Einstein" again. The computation happens entirely on encrypted data.
📊 Data State at Each Stage
  • Stage 1-3: PII exists in plaintext (temporary, in-memory only)
  • Encryption Layer: PII split into cryptographic shares
  • MPC Zone: Only encrypted shares exist, never combined
  • Output: Deterministic, irreversible anonymized token

MPC Deep Dive

How Multi-Party Computation protects privacy

🔐 Security Guarantees
  • No Single Point of Failure: Even if 1 node is compromised, the secret remains safe
  • Threshold Security: Need at least 2/3 nodes to reconstruct the original
  • Verifiable Computation: Solana blockchain ensures nodes execute correctly
  • Information-Theoretic Security: Shares reveal zero information about the original
🎯 Why This Matters
Traditional systems require you to trust a single party with your sensitive data. With Arcium's MPC, trust is distributed across multiple independent nodes. Even if some nodes are malicious or compromised, your data remains protected. This is the fundamental paradigm shift that makes privacy-preserving computation practical.
